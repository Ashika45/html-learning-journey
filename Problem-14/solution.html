Making Butter Chicken & Panner Naan

Ingredients

  • chicken
  • butter
  • cream
  • spices
  • paneer
  • maida
  • yeast
  • salt
  • sugar
  • onion
  • tomato

Steps to make Butter Chicken & Panner Naan

  1. Marinate the chicken with spices and yogurt and keep it aside for 30 minutes.
  2. Grill the chicken until it is cooked through and has a nice char on the outside.
  3. In a pan, melt butter and sauté onions until they are golden brown.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until they are soft and mushy.
  5. Add cream and spices to the pan and let it simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Add the grilled chicken to the pan and let it cook in the sauce for a few more minutes.
  7. Serve hot with naan
  8. To make naan, mix maida, yeast, salt, and sugar. Let it rise for 30 minutes. Roll out and cook on a griddle.
  9. Stuff the naan with paneer and cook until the paneer is melted and the naan is golden brown.

Enjoy your delicious Butter Chicken and Paneer Naan!
